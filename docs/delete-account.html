Metakai Privacy Terms

Delete your data

Metakai keeps your logs on your phone. The only account data on a Metakai server is an optional leaderboard profile.

Leaderboard profile

A leaderboard profile holds your display name, country, rounded body buckets (sex, age group, weight class, height band), scores, run times and friend links. Deleting it removes all of these from the server immediately.

In the app: Progress → Leaderboards → profile icon → Leave leaderboards.

Online, if you joined on Android with Google:

Delete my leaderboard profile

If you joined on iPhone with Apple: delete the profile in the app as above. You can also stop Metakai from using your Apple ID at account.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → Sign in with Apple. Profiles that upload no scores for 12 months are deleted automatically.

Data on your phone

Open Metakai and go to You → Manage data → Erase all data, or uninstall the app. This deletes your logs, photos and settings from the phone.

Google Drive backup

In the app, go to You → Backup & sync → Disconnect Google Drive → Delete backup. Without the app, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Metakai → Options → Delete hidden app data.
